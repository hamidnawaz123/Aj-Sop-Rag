## SOP for Paper Cone Sorter

1. Separate godowns have been assigned for the paper cones of Mill No. 1 and Mill No. 2. It is the responsibility of the Paper Cone Sorter to pay special attention to sampling in their respective godown and keep each party's paper cones separate according to color, ensuring required paper cones can be retrieved easily at the time of issuance.
2. The Paper Cone Sorter will prepare issue notes in accordance with daily requirements from the respective mills, ensure timely issuance, and record the entry in their register as per the issue.
3. It is the responsibility of the Paper Cone Sorter to obtain the signature of the receiver on the issue note at the time of issuance and hand over the issue note to the Main Store Packing Incharge.
4. The Paper Cone Sorter is responsible for receiving rejected paper cones, preparing detailed lists, and providing code-wise data to the Packing Incharge. Rejected paper cones must be kept separately according to party/vendor, so that all rejected material can be returned to the respective party.
5. It is the responsibility of the Paper Cone Sorter to maintain a Stock Register, recording all paper cone types and colors separately.
6. Excess issued paper cones are returned from accounts/production at times. The Paper Cone Sorter is responsible for taking all returned paper cones back into stock and informing the Store Packing Incharge accordingly.
7. Creating party-wise rejection reports and separating/returning goods by party is the responsibility of the Paper Cone Sorter.

### Reports
1. Preparing stock reports for all paper cones and maintaining the stock register.

### Accountability
1. The Paper Cone Sorter will be held responsible in case of any shortage/deficit in paper cones.
2. If paper cones are damaged due to rain or any other reason, the Paper Cone Sorter will be held responsible.
3. The Paper Cone Sorter will be directly responsible for any damage caused during sampling due to negligence.

2. It is the responsibility of the Blow Room Foreman to protect all tools and ensure weekly cleaning of tools to maximize their usability.

## SOP for Waste Receipt and Dispatch


1. The Waste Clerk daily at 7:00 AM along with the Security Guard and relevant Mill Lab representative — which for Mill No. 1 and 3 will be from the mixing side (Mixing Incharge or Senior Assistant Spinning Master), and for Mill No. 2 from the mixing side (Mixing/Packing Incharge) — will weigh the quality-wise waste on the scale in the Blow Room. The representative of each separate waste, Waste Clerk, and Lab representative will record the weight in their respective registers.
2. The Lab representative will enter all weights in duplicate books, obtain the signature of the Waste Clerk, and sign it themselves. The Lab representative will hand over one copy to the Waste Clerk and file one copy with themselves.
3. During weighing, special care must be taken that no waste mixes with another quality waste. Simplex and Ring bobbins or any other usable waste must not go outside. Special care must be taken that Pneumafil, Card Fly from error, Drawing, Sweeping, or AC filters do not get mixed.
4. Special care must be taken that at the time of receiving waste, no waste is wet; in case of wet waste, the waste section/department will be sent back. If expensive waste is mixed with cheap waste, GM Mill must be informed immediately.
5. After weighing of waste is completed, transport the bales from the place to the Waste Bale Press. All types of waste must be kept at their designated places before and after pressing, and special care must be taken during shifting that no waste mixes with another and before pressing they are at a distance from each other.
6. Keep the Bale Press area clean and shift bales on a daily basis. Check each bale so it can be determined whether different quality waste is not bound inside the bale. Pay attention to cleanliness of all relevant areas and get them cleaned by the relevant staff.
7. If mixed waste is received from a section, return the waste section back and inform GM Mill.
8. After weighing on the Blow Room gate, waste will be shifted to the Waste Bale Press and bales of each waste will be made. After making the bale, the weight of the bale, bale number, and date will be written on the bale and all details will be noted in the register simultaneously. If any waste bale, after being made, is weighed again and pressed on the Blow Room scale, check the weight against the given proforma below. If the difference is less than 1%, inform the relevant GM, and if the difference is higher, report to the CEO in head office.

## SOP — Gate Clerk

### Responsibilities

1. Inspect and verify all items received at the main gate, checking them count-wise against the unloading delivery.
2. Prepare Inward Gate Pass.
3. Maintain a record of the painting and repair of all mill vehicles in the relevant registers.
4. Maintain a record of returnable items.
5. Maintain a record of outgoing items.
6. Have all incoming items inspected by the relevant person and hand over the approved items to the store.

### Procedure

1. When any item arrives at the mill from outside, it will first be counted. It will be inspected according to the challan bill, and after verification, an Inward Gate Pass will be prepared. The gate clerk will sign the Inward Gate Pass.
2. If any discrepancy is found compared to the bill or challan, inform the Purchase Department at Head Office and prepare the Gate Pass based on the received quantity.
3. Whenever a vehicle leaves through the main gate, the gate clerk must ensure its exit. He will record the vehicle number, time of exit, and return time in the relevant register. If work such as repair or painting was performed on the vehicle, details of the work nature and cost will be entered. At the end of the month, total expenses for all vehicles' paint and work done by each individual will be detailed. A summary will be prepared by the Office Superintendent, signed by the Admin Manager and Technical Director, with one copy kept at the main gate, one sent to Accounts, and one sent to Head Office.
4. If items need to be sent out of the mill, the Main Store Gate Pass along with packed items will be handed over to the Main Gate Clerk. It is the responsibility of the Gate Clerk to send heavy items via freight stand and light items via courier. Details of returnable items along with Gate Pass No., Bilty Receipt No., and relevant register entries will be made. Upon return, an Inward Gate Pass will be generated based on old register entries.
5. Only electronic cards will be sent via courier, and a separate register is available for this at the main gate. Similarly, a register is maintained by the Office Superintendent. Both (Office Superintendent and Gate Clerk) will enter sending and receiving details in their respective registers.
6. Any yarn bag going out of the main gate will be recorded by the Gate Clerk in the relevant register.
7. After completion of inspection, approved items will be handed over to the main store along with the Inward Gate Pass. Unapproved items will be issued an Outward Gate Pass by the Main Store and sent back to the party through the Gate Clerk, who will enter the details in the relevant register.
8. If any item is borrowed from another mill, note it down in a separate register.

### Accountability

1. The Gate Clerk will be responsible for handing over the complete and entire goods along with the Inward Gate Pass to the Main Store.
2. If an Inward Gate Pass is not prepared for any goods, the Gate Clerk will be held answerable in that case.
3. The Gate Clerk will be responsible for making any incorrect entry.
4. The Gate Clerk will be held answerable for any late inspection of goods.
